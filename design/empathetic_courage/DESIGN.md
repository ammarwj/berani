## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered specifically for vulnerable youth and students (aged 10–18), educational staff, and school counselors dealing with anti-bullying awareness and psychological safety. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
The emotional goal is dual-core: **Uncompromised Safety & Reassurance** alongside **Warm Empowerment**. For an adolescent reporting distress, the visual environment must never feel punitive, cold, corporate, or overly bureaucratic. Instead, the interface acts as a calming harbor—approachable, gentle, non-judgmental, yet distinctly dependable and structurally secure.

### Chosen Design Aesthetic
The visual direction blends **Warm Modern Minimalism** with **Gentle Tactile Surfaces**:
- **Generous Visual Breathing Room**: Layouts emphasize clarity, minimal cognitive overhead, and low visual anxiety. High-stress actions (such as confidential incident reporting) employ progressive disclosure to avoid overwhelming the user.
- **Friendly Rounded Structuralism**: Broad, pillowy corner radii (`rounded-2xl` / 16px to 24px) paired with soft translucent panels counteract institutional stiffness.
- **Supportive Chromatic Accents**: Oceanic blues anchor authority and stability, counterbalanced by nurturing mint-teal and encouraging amber tones that guide reflection and learning without alarming the student.

## Colors

The color palette is calibrated to eliminate clinical coldness while preserving high contrast and accessibility compliance (WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA) across both sunny schoolyards and low-light personal mobile environments.

### Role Allocation
- **Primary (`#2563EB` - Oceanic Azure)**: The bedrock of agency, trust, and structural interface elements. Anchors key action points, verified badges, active navigation states, and primary CTA buttons.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` - Nurturing Mint)**: Symbolizes personal growth, completed reflections, positive reinforcement, and validation states across modules.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Warm Amber)**: Embodies courage, active streak highlights, scenario hints, and non-threatening alerts.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate Neutral)**: Balanced cool gray delivering crisp typography without the harshness of pure ink black.

### Contextual Named Palette
- **Surfaces**: `surface-canvas` (`#F8FAFC`) grounds the viewport in a soft, eye-resting light tint, while `surface-card` (`#FFFFFF`) lifts elevated modules cleanly off the ground.
- **Emotional States**: `support-teal` (`#06B6D4`) provides a secondary serene tone for guided breathing or mood-tracking flows. `danger-rose` (`#EF4444`) with `danger-subtle` (`#FEF2F2`) is strictly reserved for urgent panic toggles or immediate escalation warnings.
- **Tinted Backdrops**: Subtle tints (`ocean-subtle`, `mint-subtle`, `amber-subtle`) are paired with their core pigments for badge backgrounds, chip selections, and contextual banners to maintain low visual friction.

## Typography

Typography balances warmth and structural clarity. 

- **Primary & Headline Face (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Selected for its approachable geometric rhythm, open apertures, and welcoming curves. It strips intimidation away from sensitive educational and reflection tasks while maintaining energetic, youth-friendly readability.
- **Label & Data Face (`Inter`)**: Deployed across form indicators, status markers, metadata labels, and security status tickers. Its rational, neutral anatomy ensures unambiguous readability for critical identifiers (such as anonymous report tracking codes).

### Scaling & Responsive Hierarchy
Headlines step down dynamically between mobile screens (<640px) and wider viewpoints to prevent accidental awkward text wrapping and maintain comfort on compact vertical viewports. Line heights are tuned generously (1.5x–1.6x for long-form educational copy) to foster sustained focus for young readers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built mobile-first, prioritizing handheld Progressive Web App (PWA) workflows for teenage students, while scaling gracefully into a centered, focused tablet and desktop console for educators and counselors.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Mobile (<640px)**: Single column with a 4-column sub-grid, `16px` (`1rem`) outer margins, and `16px` gutters. Critical actions live comfortably within thumb zones, framed above a fixed bottom navigation bar (height: 64px + safe area inset).
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `24px` (`1.5rem`) margins and gutters. Reflector and training modules adopt split dual-pane layouts.
- **Desktop (≥1024px)**: Max content container constrained to `1120px` (or `1280px` for admin tables) centered with `40px` (`2.5rem`) outer margin across 12 fluid columns, preventing sprawling horizontal line lengths that impair reading concentration.

### Spacing Cadence
All layout distances follow an incremental 4px / 8px scale:
- `space-xs` (4px) & `space-sm` (8px): Icon-to-text lockups, badge insets, inline list item spacing.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard form element vertical gaps, interior card padding, stack gaps.
- `space-lg` (24px): Card exterior spacing, block transitions, module headers.
- `space-xl` (32px) & `space-2xl` (48px): Major section separators within educational journeys.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Calm Ambient Depth** combining soft tinted shadows and layered tonal containers. Heavy drop shadows and stark black borders are avoided to prevent visual stress.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: `surface-canvas` (`#F8FAFC`). Flat background layer.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks)**: `surface-card` (`#FFFFFF`) sitting on Canvas. Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`. Used for interactive modules, lesson list items, and article cards.
- **Level 2 (Floating Action & Navigations)**: Mobile bottom navigation bar, floating feedback panels, and sticky header bars. Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.94)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and subtle outline `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`. Shadow: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.06), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Crisis Dialogs)**: Urgent confirmation dialogs, mood reflection pickers, and anonymous ticket modals. Shadow: `0 20px 35px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Accompanied by a calming translucent backdrop overlay (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)` with `blur(4px)`).

## Shapes

The design system embraces high corner curvature to evoke gentleness, safety, and modern tactile friendliness.

### Corner Radius System
- **Base Components (`rounded-lg` / 8px - 12px)**: Input fields, dropdown triggers, and sub-action buttons.
- **Primary Interactive Cards (`rounded-2xl` / 16px - 20px)**: Content cards, learning modules, scenario response tiles, and anonymous tracking panels.
- **Pill Elements (`rounded-full` / 9999px)**: Status badges, category pills, avatar holders, primary bottom navigation icons, and active floating action controls.

### Visual Edge Treatment
Borders remain soft and feather-light: standard strokes measure exactly `1px` using `border-subtle` (`#E2E8F0`). Under active focus, rings expand by `3px` using semi-transparent `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)` to ensure accessibility without sharp visual jarring.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid fill with `primary_color_hex` (`#2563EB`), text in pure white (`#FFFFFF`), `12px` vertical / `20px` horizontal padding, `rounded-xl` (12px), `label-lg` weight. Subtle hover elevation and soft glow (`0 4px 12px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.24)`).
- **Secondary / Supportive Button**: Tinted background (`#EFF6FF`), text in `#2563EB`, border `1px solid #BFDBFE`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary Button**: Transparent background, text in `text-muted` (`#64748B`), hover text in `text-primary` (`#0F172A`) with `#F1F5F9` background.
- **Crisis / Sensitive Action Button**: Filled with `danger-rose` (`#EF4444`) with white label, reserved for urgent reporting submissions or escalation triggers.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `4px` vertical / `10px` horizontal padding, uppercase `label-sm`.
  - *Diterima / Pending*: Amber background (`#FFFBEB`), text `#D97706`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`.
  - *Diproses / In Review*: Ocean subtle (`#EFF6FF`), text `#2563EB`, border `1px solid #BFDBFE`.
  - *Selesai / Resolved*: Mint subtle (`#ECFDF5`), text `#059669`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`.
- **Category Filter Chips**: Pill-shaped, light background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, selectable state transitions into `#2563EB` fill with white text.

### Inputs & Text Areas
- Built for thumb accessibility: minimum height `48px`, border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, background `#FFFFFF`, `rounded-xl` (12px), text `body-md`.
- **Focus State**: Border shifts to `#2563EB` with an ambient ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.
- **Helper & Safety Text**: Accompanied by soothing descriptive text (`body-sm` in `#64748B`) directly below inputs to clarify why specific details are requested.

### Anonymous Mode Toggle Switch
- A specialized prominent switch component. When active, shifts the form boundary color to `support-teal` (`#06B6D4`) with a floating reassurance pill: *"Identitasmu 100% Dirahasiakan & Terlindungi"*.
- Track size: 52px × 30px with smooth spring transition. Thumb: 24px pure white disk with gentle drop shadow.

### Cards & Learning Modules
- Surfaces built on `surface-card` (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-2xl` (16px), bordered with `1px solid #E2E8F0`, padding `20px` to `24px`.
- **Interactive Scenarios**: Scenario choice tiles feature prominent letter indicators, full-width hit targets, and progressive state illumination (green for constructive responses, amber for review points).

### Reflection & Mood Tracker
- Dedicated discrete selector using 5 custom-designed empathetic facial vectors or emotional gradient pills ranging from "Sangat Cemas" to "Sangat Tenang", using `rounded-2xl` cards with tactile pressed states (`scale: 0.97`).

### Report Tracking Ticket Banner
- Unique high-security card style: soft teal gradient border (`linear-gradient(135deg, #06B6D4, #2563EB)`), monospace ticket ID with high-contrast copy button, and reassurance notice regarding non-traceable access.
